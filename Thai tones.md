## 2026-11-12

	- yim3 lai51 a, mœng41: what was that smile about?
	- pomg me de yim24: i didn't smile
	- mœng yim251: you so clearly did!!
	- okay to me this is very strong evidence that there are two rising tones:
		- the one that starts low and *sometimes* rises like sång114, sam114 (very similar to mandarin 3rd tone)
		- the one that starts level-ish and often (but not always) goes up and comes back down if exaggerated (very similar to mandarin 2nd tone)
	- so the model would be:
		- mid rising: yim25 (and probably many other known words)
		- low, maybe rise: sång114, sam114
		- high falling: hæ41, gau41
		- low falling (short-ish): si21
		- mid falling (or just long): neng32, bæt32
		- level (short): hok3, dyet3, sit3

		- 25 -> 2nd tone
		- 114 -> 3rd tone
